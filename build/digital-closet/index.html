Case Study — Digital Feature

My Closet

A digital closet feature showing customers the apparel they already own — built to increase Member engagement in record time.

Pedro Björn · 5 months concept-to-delivery (company record)

"How might we bring back our customers using clothes they already own?"

Customer needs

"I want to look and feel good" — untapped potential in clothes already bought from us

Business goals

Increase engagement (return rate, session length, pages/session); relevance (add-to-cart, conversion); sustainability via building on existing apparel

Approach

Leverage existing data (purchase history); 5-month delivery target

Exploration

Research

6 formative interviews about customers' real closets → categorised into 4 Jobs-to-be-done:

01

I want to look and feel good

02

I want my wardrobe to last

03

I want to keep my wardrobe organised

04

I want to get rid of my clothes

Competitive Analysis

12 apps/services covering these needs

Ideation

2 Design Studios:

  • (1) Blue-sky ideation, 11 participants, affinity mapping → backlog
  • (2) Cluster and refine sketches, vote on best

Customer Quotes

"I run out of space — I get rid of clothes"

"When I have time I organise it and make it easier to find"

"It'll be a mini closet which will show clothes I like or I'd like to try"

Discovery

Refinement: Defined user-flow + basic functionality → fast prototype

Flows

01 Login

Closet accessed via existing Sign-up/-in flow; expectations set before and after login; empty state routes new shoppers to store immediately

Login Flow Mockup
(HTML/CSS Component)

02 Closet View

All previously purchased items gathered, categorised; swipable product sliders (clothes-hanger feel — clothing shown without details); intro message; vertically scrollable categories; filterable + sortable

Closet View - Hanger Slider
(HTML/CSS Component - Signature Piece)

03 Details

Tap product → bottom sheet with image gallery, product info, options: care information, styling recommendations, buy again

Details Flow Mockup
(HTML/CSS Component)

Validation

5 usability studies (desirability + time-on-task) → refined final design; backlog additions: tag/favourite items, add items not bought from us

Study Quotes:

  • "It's showing me what's in my wardrobe"
  • "I can narrow down to just see my trousers"
  • "I can look how they look together comparing"
  • "I don't think I'd ever use this feature"

Delivery

01

All components built on the established Design System

02

Developers already embedded throughout, clean handoff

03

Live study on production feature, learnings documented for next iteration

Outcome

0% session length increase
↑ sign-in correlation from Closet page
5 mo concept-to-delivery

Lessons

Win

  • Validated stickiness
  • Record delivery

Hard truth

  • Feature heavy/expensive on backend
  • Only a portion of customers' wardrobes represented → limited usefulness
  • Flagged early to stakeholders
  • Served as learning MVP
  • Sunsetted after one year

A feature measured not just by what it achieved, but by what it taught the team.
