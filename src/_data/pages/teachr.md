## Problem & Goal (Why!?) | Problem Definition | problem

The learning experience faced a number of issues, from registration errors to slow training times.

This was due to a variety of factors, among which was the inconsistency of the experience making it very difficult to modify and add to the experience.

The goal was to streamline and unify the experience in order to reduce human errors and speed up training whilst maintaining learning quality.

### Users and Needs

Users of our learning tools tended to be factory workers, and had low to medium digital literacy, and in some extreme cases our app was the first software they'd ever seen.

They also needed to do learning while on the job, and thus had very little time to use our product.

Their needs and the needs of their supervisors are different, but they all share urgency to get the learning over with few errors.

## Research

Aside from product performance metrics we also spoke with workplace managers and participants to get their perspectives.

Our findings could be summarised as:

- Registration was confusing, with a lot of information on screen at a time, making it hard to solve errors quickly.
- The lessons were slow and took time, jumping between videos, questions, and a map, which made it hard to know what to do next.
- There wasn't a lot of information about certain lessons, making it awkward to navigate things like training in groups, switching lessons, etc.

## Discovery & Validation | Discovery | discovery

With the goal in mind, we decided to prioritize unifying the flow, ensuring registration and lessons used similar, simple and recognisable UX patterns.
