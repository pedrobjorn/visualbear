# DR-0006: nav options are natural-width and spread edge-to-edge in the content-width row; labels condensed to single simple terms

- Date: 2026-11-24
- Status: accepted
- Supersedes: the equal-fill reading of DR-0001/DR-0005 at the BUTTON level (`li { flex: 1 1 0 }` + `a.btn { width: 100% }`). DR-0005 stands at the bar/row level: the bar stays full-bleed, the `ul` row stays constrained to the body content width.
- Context: Pedro 2026-11-24: "adapt the buttons inside cs-index to be better organised: left most and right most options touch the edges. Don't cut off the button widths. instead just use simple terms like Deepdive, etc". Measured on the DR-0005 build (evidence/t5-verify.md captures): equal-fill buttons clip their labels mid-word at 1440 ("»ISCOVERY & VALIDATION", "EPDIVE: TEACHR ASSIST…" in 176.8px buttons) and collapse into an unreadable strip at 390 (71.6px buttons). Forcing equal widths is what cuts the labels off.
- Decision: buttons keep their NATURAL width (`li` back to plain `display: flex`, no `flex: 1 1 0`, no `min-width: 0`; `a.btn` drops `width: 100%`), and the `ul` distributes them with `justify-content: space-between` - so the leftmost option touches the row's left edge and the rightmost touches the right edge, with the middle options spaced between. The row itself is limited to the content CONTAINER's width: `width: 916px` (= `$content-width`, the `.document-text` border box) + `max-width: 100%` (below 900 the container is full-width), centered in the full-bleed bar via `margin: 0 auto` - row edges 262/1178 at 1440, 0/768 at 768, 0/390 at 390. No button is narrower than its label, so nothing is cut; `overflow-x: auto` on the `ul` remains the fallback when the natural row exceeds the row width (390). Labels are condensed to single simple terms in the per-page `nav:` lists (e.g. teachr "Deepdive: Teachr Assistant" -> "Deepdive", "Discovery & Validation" -> "Discovery", "Design & Delivery" -> "Design"; fashion "Future & Learnings" -> "Future"; sports "Brand & Process" -> "Process", "Site Redesign" -> "Redesign"; closet "Flows & Validation" -> "Flows", "Design System" -> "Design"; teachr-agent already simple, unchanged). The anchor keeps `overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis; white-space: nowrap` + `title` as a no-op safety edge (at natural width it never triggers). Bar mechanics unchanged (full-bleed, sticky top 48, 35px, gap cover->nav 0).
- Rationale: "don't cut off the button widths" rules out forced equal widths; "left most and right most touch the edges" is exactly `space-between` in the container-width row; "simple terms" keeps the natural row within the container at desktop so the row reads as one organized strip instead of clipped equal cells.
- Consequences: `_case-study.scss` `ul` gains `justify-content: space-between` (replaces center) and `width: 916px; max-width: 100%;` (Pedro's edit: the container border box, not the 884px content box), `li` loses `flex: 1 1 0`/`min-width: 0`, `a.btn` loses `width: 100%` (ellipsis trio + title stay). The 4 changed drafts (`fashion-rebrand`, `sports-ecommerce-redesign`, `digital-closet`, `teachr`) get new `Nav:` labels and their page mds are regenerated via `draft-to-md.js` (round-trip byte-identical modulo the pre-existing teachr quote normalization, goldens PASS, verify-page-md gate passes - all new labels are well under the 28-char cap). `cs-nav.test.js`: `PAGE_NAV` lists updated; CSS contract flips to `space-between` on ul + `width: 916px` + `max-width: 100%`, NO `flex: 1 1 0`/`min-width: 0` on li, NO `width: 100%` on a.btn, ellipsis/`title=`/full-bleed assertions kept. Playwright AC: ul left/right == `.document-text` border-box left/right within 0.5px (262/1178 at 1440, 0/768, 0/390); first anchor left == ul left and last anchor right == ul right within 0.5px at 1440/768; no anchor clipped at 1440/768 (anchor scrollWidth <= clientWidth + 1); at 390 page scrollWidth <= 390, first anchor flush left, no anchor clipped; li computed flex-grow == 0; bar still full-bleed, gap cover->nav == 0, stuck top 48 + header bg, bar height 35.
