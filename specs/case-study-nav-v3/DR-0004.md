# DR-0004: The nav label cap is 28 chars, not the proposed 24

- Date: 2026-11-24
- Status: accepted
- Amends: DR-0003 (cap value only; the per-page lists and the gate itself stand)
- Context: DR-0003 proposed a 24-char label cap as an ASSUMPTION. teachr's configured v2 list contains "Deepdive: Teachr Assistant" (26 chars), which Pedro wrote explicitly on 2026-11-24; a 24 cap would force editing his list or a grandfather exception, and the cap's job is only to stop full section titles (fashion's "Future Improvements, Based on Learnings" is 41 chars) from becoming links.
- Decision: `NAV_LABEL_MAX = 28` in `verify-page-md.js` gate 1b. 28 admits every proposed condensed label (longest: "Flows & Validation" / "Future & Learnings" = 18) and Pedro's 26-char teachr label, while rejecting full-title labels (>= 29 chars).
- Rationale: one constant, no exceptions list; the cap guards against regression (long titles), not against Pedro's own wording.
- Consequences: `verify-page-md.js` gate 1b fails any `nav:` label over 28 chars; `cs-nav.test.js` asserts the rejection on a synthetic fixture; AUTHORING.md documents 28.
