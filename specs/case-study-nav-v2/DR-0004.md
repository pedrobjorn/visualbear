# DR-0004: The section nav sits directly under the hero cover, before the `.cs-hero--meta` band

- Date: 2026-11-24
- Status: accepted
- Context: Pedro 2026-11-24 (follow-up): "Move the nav bar directly under the hero." Today `mdHero` (markdown-render.js:1401) emits the cover `<header class="cs-hero cs-hero--teal">` and the meta band `<section class="cs-hero cs-hero--meta">` as ONE string, and the 5 case-study templates render `mdhero` -> `mdtoc` -> `mdpage` - so the nav lands after the meta band. Measured 2026-11-24 (built teachr, 1440x900): DOM order `header.cs-hero` -> `section.cs-hero--meta` -> `nav.cs-index`; the cover's bottom is at 443.6px and the nav's top at 1189.5px - a 745.8px meta band sits between them (1268.7px at 390x844). Options considered: (a) move the nav template line above the meta by splitting the hero emission; (b) move the meta band below the nav by making `mdHero` emit meta separately; (c) leave order, restyle only.
- Decision: (a)+(b) are the same change done cleanly: add two filters - `mdcover(src)` returning only the cover `<header class="cs-hero...">`, and `mdherometa(src)` returning `heroMetaHtml(meta.hero.meta)` (the existing internal builder, markdown-render.js:1374) - registered in gulp/nunjucks.js next to `mdhero`. `mdhero` itself stays byte-identical (blog x4, /workflow/, styleguide keep cover+meta in one call - non-goal). The 5 case-study templates (fashion-rebrand, sports-ecommerce-redesign, digital-closet, teachr, teachr-agent) render:
  ```
  {{ site.data.pages['<slug>'] | mdcover | safe }}
  {{ site.data.pages['<slug>'] | mdtoc | safe }}
  {{ site.data.pages['<slug>'] | mdherometa | safe }}
  {{ site.data.pages['<slug>'] | mdpage | safe }}
  ```
  DOM order becomes `header.cs-hero` -> `nav.cs-index` -> `section.cs-hero--meta` -> `.cs-section`s, with zero gap between the cover's bottom edge and the nav's top edge in flow.
- Rationale: "the hero" is the hero cover; the nav is the page's index and belongs directly under the title block, so it reaches its stuck position sooner and the meta band (needs/metrics) reads as content below the nav, not between hero and nav. Splitting the emission (instead of reordering inside `mdhero`) keeps every non-case-study `mdhero` consumer byte-identical.
- Consequences: `cs-nav.test.js` position assertion changes from "after the hero, before the first `.cs-section`" to "immediately after the cover `header.cs-hero`'s closing tag and before `section.cs-hero--meta`"; Playwright asserts `nav.cs-index.getBoundingClientRect().top === header.cs-hero.getBoundingClientRect().bottom` at page top (gap 0) at 1440 and 390; sticky behavior unchanged (`top: 48px` once scrolled, the meta band scrolls under the stuck bar like any content); AUTHORING.md's shell description updates to the four-line order; the built-HTML assertion "nav before first `.cs-section`" still holds.
