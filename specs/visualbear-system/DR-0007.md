# DR-0007: The robot's hover keeps only the pill (supersedes the plate half of DR-0006/ticket 15)

## Context

DR-0006/ticket 15 rounded the hover plate (999px). Pedro's follow-on drops
the plate outright: "Remove the red background to the robot hover, just
leave the pill."

## Decision

- The robot link carries NO background plate in any state - at rest, hover
  and focus. The expanded label chip (`a.robot .robot-pill`, the solid
  `$vb-light` plate + `$vb-dark` ink per T10/13) is the only surface.
- The link's own plate comes from the GLOBAL `a` rule (`a:hover/:focus`
  background-color `#d73735`); the override is a robot-scoped
  `background-color: transparent` on those states.
- `border-radius: 999px` (T15) stays - inert without a plate, and guards
  any future surface from regressing to a square.
- Everything else of T10/T13 unchanged: the 44x44 hit box, the glyph
  position, the pill geometry, aria wiring, reduced motion, idempotent
  strip.

Rejected: replacing the plate with a ring/ghost outline - Pedro asked for
the pill ONLY.

## Verification note

Probe computed `background-color` on the link in all three states:
transparent / transparent / transparent while the pill still expands
(rendered width >= 200px).
