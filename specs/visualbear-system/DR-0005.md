# DR-0005: The amendment fixes to tickets 6, 8 and 10 (three regressions, 2026-11-12 after Pedro's review)

## Decision

Three regressions found by Pedro on the built mirror ("selected works"):

1. **The Design system tab renders inside the case study's column** (ticket 6 shipped the panel inside `#workflow_container > .gallery-container > .container > .document-text.text-box`). Consequences measured at 1440:

   - panel content width 836 inside the case-study text-box; the styleguide page's same paragraphs render identical (836/680/836) BUT the panel's `img` renders **512px** (the `.text-box img` legacy cap) where the styleguide renders **836px** - the component-preview art is visibly shrunk;
   - `.case-study.cs-workflow`'s forced dark-ground component instances (`--cs-chip-plate` white 0.04, muted #a6adb8 notes, white 0.06 surfaces) leak into the light panel, breaking the case-study previews that the Design system section exists to show;
   - the whole view reads "a narrow container", which is what Pedro flagged.

   **Fix:** the design panel renders as the styleguide page renders - as a full-width section directly under the page (`section.section.section-top-padding#styleguide_container > article.text-container > div.row > blocks`), outside the cs-workflow article and its text-box, exactly mirroring `src/styleguide/index.nunjucks`'s structure. The panel give-back CSS from T6 (`.case-study.cs-workflow .vb-panel--designsystem` inks/bg pin) retires - the panel no longer sits inside that scope, so no ink fight exists. The tab controller ([data-vb-panel] query) is position-agnostic; keyboard/roving wiring unchanged.

2. **The robot pill is clipped to a 28px sliver** (ticket 10). The pill is a flex item inside the fixed 44x44 anchor; `flex-shrink: 1` (default) crushes it against the glyph - computed `max-width: 220px` but **rendered width 28px** at hover (text scrollWidth 213). The T10 verification probed computed max-width, not rendered geometry - the gate missed it. **Fix:** `flex-shrink: 0` on `.robot-pill`; the expanded pill then overflows the 44px box to the right (anchor overflow is visible), which is the growth direction the ticket pinned (toward the page center). Collapsed stays zero-width. `max-width` raised to 260px (the string + padding needs ~241px at 13px bold; 220 clipped the tail). The solid chip (DR-0005 context: grounds vary under a fixed element) is kept - it was the right call; the clipping was the break.

3. **The mirror's About link navigates to `about/`** (ticket 8 mapped `/#about` -> depth-relative `about/`). The site's About is not a page visit - the public nav's `/#about` scrolls to the home's `#about` section, and Pedro wants that anchor behavior preserved ("anchor down to the anchor section under"). **Fix:** `relativizeNavLinks` maps `/#about` -> depth-relative mirror home + `#about` (depth 0: `#about` in-page, depth 1: `../#about`). The mirror keeps shipping `about/` (unchanged, unreachable by nav) - removing it is out of scope and would need its own decision.

## Rejected alternatives

- Panel breakout via CSS (negative margins/100vw) - rejected: fighting the case-study column in CSS keeps every ink-leak exception in tabs.scss forever; moving the panel out of the scope removes the problem at the markup level, where ticket 6's intent ("the panel IS the styleguide page") lives.
- About -> `about/` page visit (T8's choice) - rejected by Pedro: inconsistent with the rest of the site, where About is an anchor, and a whole-page visit for what the home already carries a section for.
- Pills as absolutely-positioned overlays - rejected: position:absolute breaks the flow-based growth transition and the touch-target probe (the link must be the 44x44 box).

## Verification notes

- T10's rendered-geometry gap closes: the evidence now records the pill's `getBoundingClientRect().width` (>= 200px expanded), not just computed max-width.
