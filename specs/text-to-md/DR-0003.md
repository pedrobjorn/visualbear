# DR-0003: The draft txt is the copy source of truth; the page md is derived

- Date: 2026-11-02
- Status: accepted (Pedro, 2026-11-02, same day as the ticket 1-6 ship)
- Context: the shipped workflow archived the processed txt and treated
  the page md as the editable source (DR-0002's idempotence guard
  refused re-conversion on hand-edited pages). Pedro's actual model is
  the opposite: "don't archive; the txt is where I control the content
  and may edit as need be. The md is just a middleground to ensure
  determinism."

## Decision

- `src/_data/drafts/<slug>.txt` is the page's LIVING copy source; one
  txt per page, permanently. No inbox, no `processed/` archive.
- `src/_data/pages/<slug>.md` is a DERIVED artifact: deterministic
  output of the text-to-md rules, committed because the renderer
  consumes it, but never hand-edited.
- Re-conversion is the normal response to a txt edit: the skill diffs
  the old md against the new and reports what changed (the diff is the
  copy-change review), then runs the verify gates.
- Copy edits always happen in the txt; hand edits to the md are drift -
  the next conversion overwrites them (by design, not by accident:
  the skill warns when it detects the md no longer matches its own
  last conversion).

## Rationale

Pedro does not want a second editing surface. One file owns the words;
determinism is the md's only job. The golden suite + canonicalizer +
verify gates already guarantee determinism, so nothing about DR-0002's
core changes - only the ownership flips.

## Consequences

- Supersedes the idempotence guard wording in DR-0002 ("re-running on
  an edited md is refused"): refusal applies only when the MD drifted
  from its last conversion without a txt change (drift, not authority).
- The skill must always show a before/after diff of the page md so a
  re-conversion is reviewable as a copy change.
- AUTHORING.md and the drafts README document the txt-as-source model.
