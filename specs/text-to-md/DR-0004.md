# DR-0004: Image-run rule — grouped images become one ::: wide band

- Date: 2026-11-09 (amended 2026-11-18: runs emit `::: wide`, not `::: shots`)
- Status: accepted
- Context: Case-study drafts carry related captures (desktop + mobile of one view, states of one flow). Until now, related images had to be grouped by hand-editing the derived page md, which DR-0003 forbids.
- Decision: In the draft, 2+ consecutive `![alt](src)` lines (no blank line, no `[caption:]` between) are one image run: the converter emits them inside one `::: wide ... :::` fence (the Wide images container: 75% of the viewport on desktop, 85% tablet, 90% mobile — see specs/teachr-case-study/DR-0003). The alt text is each shot's caption. A solo image keeps the existing behavior: it pairs with a following `[caption:]` as a showcase figure. (`::: shots` remains available as the full-bleed band container.)
- Rationale: Grouping is structure, not content - it belongs to the deterministic converter, not to hand edits of the derived md. The signal is pure layout (adjacency), needs no new syntax, and reads naturally in the plain draft.
- Consequences: To un-group, insert a blank line or prose between the images. Inside a band the shots share one height (per-shot flex-grow = image aspect ratio) and the per-image showcase tiers (showcase-tier DR) don't apply. Implemented in `specs/text-to-md/tools/draft-to-md.js`; documented in the skill's [rule].
