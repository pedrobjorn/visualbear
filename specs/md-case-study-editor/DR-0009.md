# DR-0009 — Case-study layout: one text edge, bleeding boxes, full-bleed tier

- Date: 2026-10-29
- Status: ACCEPTED (Pedro approved the bleeding-boxes look: "the boxes can
  live within the bleed"; full-bleed tier requested same day)
- Supersedes: the ticket-18 "tier system" (multiple indent tiers)

## Decision

The case-study layout has exactly **two horizontal containers** and one
optional extension:

1. **The text edge** — every body-level element (paragraphs, list text,
   boxed content, timeline step text) aligns here. One edge, ±2px.
2. **The bleed** — boxes (cards, fact chips, summary, quote-group cards)
   extend one `$cs-indent` (28px) step into each gutter past the text
   edge; their text stays on the text edge. Below `$break-small` the
   bleed and padding shrink to 16px.
3. **The full-bleed tier** (opt-in and default for media) — images
   (showcases), tables and background-flagged containers span the full
   viewport width (`100vw` technique, padding 0), edge to edge.

Markers (list dots, annotation dots, timeline numbers) hang in the
gutter, outside the text edge. Quotes are display elements everywhere
(centered, italic, h2-size) — never plain paragraphs. The hero summary
box is a display element (centered text in the centered hero).

## Why

Five measured left edges for body-level content (345/361/373/381/389)
made the pages unscannable; body copy scattered across sizes. One edge
plus bleeding boxes keeps the calm editorial rhythm while letting
components keep their containers. Full-bleed exists because media and
tables were "wider but not wide" (the cs-wide wrapper's 48px inset) — a
half-measure that read as a bug.

## Consequences

- `$cs-indent` is the single spacing token for the indent system.
- The bare `nav` element styles had to be scoped (`nav.site-nav`) — any
  future `<nav>` must not inherit the fixed site-header styles.
- Full-bleed images upscale small sources; authors should use
  reasonably large images (noted in AUTHORING.md).

## Amendment (2026-10-29, ticket 19): bullets may take a slight indent

Bullet lists (plain `ul` in sections, boxes and comparison panels) sit at
text edge + 8px: the dot marker starts on the text edge, the text
indents 8px. The timeline (numbered steps) and annotation points keep
text on the edge with the marker in the gutter — they are step/point
markers, not bullet lists. The edge-audit tool allows list items at
edge + 8px ±2 as well as the edge itself.

## Amendment (2026-10-31, tickets 30–32): the bleed is capped by the container inset

The 28px bleed is only safe while the container's side inset is ≥ 28px.
Measured (Playwright, 2026-10-31): the `.document-text` inset is 16px at
768 and only exceeds 28px from ~791 up, so at viewports 768–~791 every
bleeding box sticks up to 12px outside the viewport — a real horizontal
scroll on all case-study pages (previously verified only at 390 and
1440, which missed the band). Below 767 boxes sit 4px from the viewport
edge, under the promised 8px margin.

Rules added:

1. The applied bleed is `min($cs-indent, container inset − 8px)` at
   every viewport — `$cs-indent` stays 28px; the _applied_ bleed shrinks
   where the inset cannot carry it. No JS.
2. Container-level tables scroll inside their full-bleed wrapper
   (`overflow-x: auto`); a table's min-content width never widens the
   page (fashion-rebrand's `::: table` measured 496px on a 390 viewport).
3. Box audits must measure boxes (not just text): viewport overflow,
   the ≥8px margin, `scrollWidth == innerWidth`, and the bleed edge —
   at 390/480/600/768/780/1024/1440. Text-only edge audits stay
   necessary but are no longer sufficient.

## Amendment (2026-10-31, tickets 25/33/34): one bullet geometry, markers stay in their box

Measured (Playwright, 2026-10-31) after Pedro's report "bullets still
don't indent but spill out of bleed; resume bullets have no spacing":

1. The ticket-19 bullet rule (dot ON the text edge, text +8px) applies
   to ALL bullets — plain lists AND fact chips. Chip plates keep their
   approved bleed; the chip's dot moves from the gutter (edge −8) onto
   the text edge, and chip text takes the same +8 indent as plain
   bullets. One bullet geometry per page, measurable as: every dot's
   left edge == the section text edge ±1px.
2. Gutter hangs are for open gutters only. Inside a box (panel, card,
   col), markers never render outside the box's edges — annotation
   dots included (measured: fashion's `[point:]` dots floated 16px
   outside their panels, and landed ON the viewport edge at 768).
3. Unmarked lists stay unmarked: the resume's name/contact/download
   exclusions must hold in the built DOM, not just in the stylesheet.

## Amendment (2026-10-31, ticket 35): 4px breathing room between bullet and text

Pedro: "spacing between bullet and text 4px gap to give it a bit more
breathing room." The +8px offset measured from the dot's LEFT edge left
only a 3px clear gap after the 5px dot. The rule becomes:

1. The dot still sits ON the text edge (unchanged).
2. The clear gap between the dot's right edge and the text's left edge
   is 4px — text at text edge + 9px (5px dot + 4px gap). Supersedes the
   edge + 8px offset from the ticket-19 amendment and build batch 1.
3. Applies to every bullet treatment: fact chips, plain lists (section
   level and in-box) and the resume's content lists. The chip's
   symmetric right padding widens by the same 1px (edge + 9).
4. The edge-audit tolerance for bullet text moves from edge + 8 ± 2 to
   edge + 9 ± 1; box-audit's dot/text checks move with it.
