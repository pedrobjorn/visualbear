# DR-0012 — One chip plate, tokenized per ground (why the pages differ)

- Date: 2026-10-31
- Status: PROPOSED — the fashion ground change is Pedro's call (vetoable)
- Context: Pedro, 2026-10-31 — "use the same chip text containers from
  ecommerce or teachr in fashion and visuAI, why are they different?"

## Why they are different (measured, 2026-10-31)

The chip plate is ONE idea — a translucent white layer lighter than the
ground — retuned per ground because no single opacity works on all of
them. Composited luminance delta, plate vs actual local ground:

| Page            | plate                     | ground          | delta |
| --------------- | ------------------------- | --------------- | ----- |
| ecommerce (ref) | rgba(255,255,255,0.45)    | #fdf0f7 pink    | 2.2   |
| teachr (ref)    | rgba(255,255,255,0.45)    | #f0f8f6 mint    | 1.5   |
| closet          | rgba(255,255,255,0.45)    | #e6f2ff blue    | 2.6   |
| fashion         | #fff solid + border (t38) | #fafafa neutral | 2.0   |
| visuAI          | rgba(255,255,255,0.06)    | #221d1f dark    | 5.4   |

- **Reference pages read via hue-neutralization**: on tinted grounds the
  plate neutralizes the tint, so even a 1.5–2.6 luminance delta has a
  perceptible soft edge.
- **fashion's ground is neutral near-white**: a translucent white has
  nothing to neutralize — the same 2.0 delta has no perceptible edge,
  which is why t38 reached for solid white + a hairline border.
- **visuAI's ground is dark**: white at 0.45 is glare (the workflow
  block's documented reason). Built at 0.06 (delta 5.4 — the most
  visible chip of the five), then tuned to 0.04 (delta 3.5) after
  Pedro's "make them match": visibly a softer plate than the light
  pages' while keeping the same lighter-than-ground read — dark
  grounds need more delta than 2.2 for the plate to read at all.

## Decision

1. The plate becomes ONE token: `--cs-chip-plate`, default
   `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)` — the reference value — consumed by
   `.cs-facts li`. Per-ground overrides are documented instances of the
   token, not separate decisions.
2. **fashion**: the ground moves `#fafafa → #f0f0f2` so the REFERENCE
   plate reads on it — MEASURED after build: delta 2.7, sitting at the
   top of the reference band (teachr 1.5, ecommerce 2.2, closet 2.6);
   borderless, literally the same value as the references. (The build
   estimate of ~7 was miscalibrated — luminance is nonlinear near
   white.) The t38 border drops. VETOABLE: if Pedro wants the #fafafa
   ground kept, the fallback is t38's solid-white + border (his
   original "brighter" ask).
3. **visuAI**: keeps the 0.06 instance — it IS the same plate on a dark
   ground; the geometry, edges, dot and text rules are already identical
   to the references (tickets 39/36).
4. Geometry is not part of this record — chips everywhere share edges
   (text column), dot-on-edge and the 8px gap (tickets 36/39).

## Consequences

- Future themes define `--cs-chip-plate` (and, if needed, the ground)
  instead of overriding `.cs-facts li` directly.
- box-audit does not gate plate contrast (luminance deltas cannot express
  hue-neutralization); the measured table above is the evidence of record.
