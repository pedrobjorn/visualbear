# DR-0005: The photography switch must stay visible above the photos: never covered by them, never an opaque bar

- Date: 2026-11-17
- Status: accepted (pending build) - amendment to Ticket 3 (DR-0003), Pedro's follow-up 2026-11-17: "the tabs should not hide behind the photos and not be opaque so they can be seen above the photos"
- Context: after T3 the switch is a sticky pill strip with the translucent chip plate (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` fallback) and transparent buttons, hiding on scroll down. Two risks when it sticks over the photogrid: (a) a scrolling photo painting OVER the strip (z-order), making the tabs literally hide behind the photos; (b) the plate becoming an opaque solid bar that blocks the photos entirely - or, at the other extreme, the nearly transparent plate over a bright photo making the label ink unreadable.
- Decision: (1) z-order guarantee - `.photoview-switch` keeps `z-index: 1` and hit-tests above the grid images (`elementFromPoint` over the stuck, visible strip returns the BUTTON, never an `img`); the lightbox dialog (z-index 1000) stays above the strip. (2) The plate stays translucent - NOT an opaque solid - but the T3 white-whisper plate (`rgba(255,255,255,0.04)`) measured ~1.0:1 label contrast over the brightest shipped photo pixels, i.e. the tabs literally hid behind bright photos. The strip now overrides `--cs-chip-plate` locally to a DARK translucent plate in the page color: `rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.92)` (measured floor over the shipped photos is alpha 0.90 for 4.5:1 on both inks; 0.92 ships for margin). At rest it blends into the `#18181b` ground; over photos the labels measure 7.68:1 (muted) / 5.88:1 (active) worst-case, and photos still ghost through at 8%. (3) The T3 scroll-down auto-hide stays: "not hide behind the photos" is read as z-order/legibility, not as removing the hide-on-scroll behavior Pedro explicitly requested earlier. Open question in plan.md asks Pedro to confirm; if he means "never auto-hide", that is a one-line removal in photoview.js.
- Rationale: tabs that float above the photos must be both on top (stacking) and see-through (translucent plate), with readable labels - the workflow tab strip already works this way.
- Consequences: verification adds an `elementFromPoint` stacking check and a label-contrast-over-photo probe at 1440/390. If the plate alpha changes, update both this DR and DR-0003's token note.
