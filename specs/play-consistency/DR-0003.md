# DR-0003: The photography view switch becomes a `.vb-tabs`-style pill strip with a plate background that hides on scroll down and returns on scroll up

- Date: 2026-11-17
- Status: accepted (pending build); partially supersedes play-subpages DR-0008
- Context: the grid/full switch (T17) is a sticky bar (`.photoview-switch`, `top: 48px`) whose buttons (`.photoview-btn`) were restyled to mirror `.vb-tab` in T21 and then given a solid `$vb-dark` background on a transparent bar in T24 (DR-0008) so stuck-over photos would not bleed through. Pedro now wants the options to have backgrounds, to disappear on scroll down and appear on scroll up, in the same style as the visuAI / Design system tabs.
- Options weighed: (a) reuse the tabs molecule markup - rejected (unchanged from DR-0007): the tabs controller boots on `[data-vb-tablist]` and would try to swap nonexistent panels; the switch stays an `aria-pressed` group and mirrors `.vb-tabs`/`.vb-tab` values, scoped to `.photoview`; (b) keep solid `$vb-dark` buttons and just add hide/show - rejected, Pedro explicitly asked for the tabs style, where the background lives on the strip plate, not on each button; (c) mirror the `.vb-tabs` strip exactly: `padding: 4px`, `border-radius: 999px`, `gap: 4px`, `width: max-content`, centered, `background-color: var(--cs-chip-plate, rgba(255,255,255,0.04))` (the dark-ground chip plate from `_case-study.scss:1388`), with `.vb-tab`-mirror buttons (transparent on the plate, inactive ink `#a6adb8`, active `#ee6e73` + underline, 44px hit box, uppercase 0.18em tracking, compact breakpoints) - chosen; (d) hide via `display: none` - rejected, it breaks the sticky layout and focus; hide via `transform + opacity + visibility` instead.
- Decision: `.photoview-switch` keeps `position: sticky; top: 48px; z-index: 1` and gains the `.vb-tabs` strip geometry + plate background, plus `transition: transform 0.2s ease, opacity 0.2s ease, visibility 0.2s` and a `.photoview-switch--hidden` state (`transform: translateY(-8px); opacity: 0; visibility: hidden` - `visibility: hidden` also removes the buttons from the tab order while hidden). `.photoview-btn` drops the `background: $vb-dark` fill and mirrors `.vb-tab` (this supersedes DR-0008's solid-button decision: the plate now provides the background that blocks scrolling photos). `photoview.js` gains a passive, rAF-throttled scroll listener: hide when scrolling down past 120px (delta > 4px), show when scrolling up (delta > 4px) or back at the top (`scrollY <= 120`), so the bar never flickers near the page top and is always visible at rest at the top. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables the transition (hide/show still applies, instantly).
- Rationale: one visual language for tab-like strips across the site (workflow tabs and the photography switch now share geometry and plate token), and the hide-on-scroll-down behavior matches Pedro's read of long photo pages: controls get out of the way while reading, return on intent to go back up.
- Consequences: the strip's inks/plate are duplicated from `tabs.scss` (same known duplication DR-0007 recorded); if the tab strip inks change, update both or promote them to tokens. DR-0008 is superseded for the button background only; its 900px content-line alignment for title and bar stays. The switch remains keyboard-reachable: when hidden it is `visibility: hidden` (not focusable), and it reappears on scroll up before any tab could reach it while hidden.
