# DR-R4-0001: Cards keep the Drive PDFs on release 4.0; the page cutover is deferred

- Date: 2026-11-02
- Status: accepted
- Context: The home-page work cards were switched from Drive PDF links to
  the new internal case-study pages (commit `8ef9c9c` line of work).
  Release 4.0 must ship all the rebuilt-page changes without switching
  what recruiters/clients are served - Pedro's brief: "the case study
  cards still point to the drive pdf files for now. We do want to keep
  these pages though in the build, just unlisted and unlinked."
- Decision: Restore the three Drive PDF hrefs (+ `_blank`) in
  `src/_data/work.json`. The three md case-study pages stay built, reach
  only by URL, and get noindex. The card->page cutover is a later,
  one-file change (`work.json` only).
- Rationale: Pedro shares the Drive PDFs today; the pages are new and not
  yet human-reviewed/publish-approved. Decoupling "ship the system" from
  "cut over the cards" keeps release 4.0 zero-risk on live content.
- Consequences: Until the cutover, two renderings of each case study
  exist (PDF live, page hidden); the page therefore needs noindex so
  crawlers never surface the newer version by accident.
