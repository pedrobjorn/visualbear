# DR-0002: corrected contract - body content sits at the nav's 16px page margin, not at the nav box edge; only image bleeds touch the viewport edge

- Date: 2026-11-15
- Status: accepted (Pedro's correction, 2026-11-15: "no, match the HOME icon
  and the text limit. not the edge of the navigation bar. take the margin of
  the nav bar and apply it to the body content. with exceptions of image
  bleeds, nothing should touch the bleed!"). Supersedes DR-0001's contract.
- Context: DR-0001 aligned body containers to the nav `.content` box edges
  (262..1178 @1440, 0..390 @390). Pedro rejected that: on mobile that puts
  body content flush with the navigation bar's edge (the screen edge). The
  nav bar itself keeps a margin inside its box - nav.scss's "16px page
  margin (edge-text rule)": the last link's label ends 16px inside the box's
  right edge (1162 @1440, 374 @390), and the home icon sits at the left
  (img 266 @1440, 12 @390).
- Decision: the nav's 16px page margin is applied to the body content.
  1. Body text and content boxes sit 16px inside the nav `.content` box on
     every device: desktop 278..1162 @1440, mobile 16..vw-16. The right edge
     matches the nav text limit exactly; the left edge sits next to the home
     icon.
  2. Containers keep the nav box width (max-width $content-width = 916,
     full-bleed below 916) but carry 16px side padding, so their content
     lands on the margin line. Gallery cards get a 16px column gutter inside
     `.content.gallery` (cards at nav box +-16). The hero text pads
     `.hero .content` by 16px (scoped to .hero so the nav's own `.content`
     is untouched).
  3. Image bleeds are the only exception: full-bleed media
     (`cs-wide--full`, showcase full tier, the photogrid) may span the
     viewport 0..vw. Nothing else may touch the bleed: on small screens the
     case-study box bleed (`-$cs-indent` = 28px) is capped at 8px below 916px
     viewport, so bleeding boxes keep 8px clearance from the screen edge
     (the old "never touch or cross the edge" rule, preserved at the new
     gutter).
- Rationale: "match the HOME icon and the text limit" = the visible nav
  elements' span, which is the nav box inset by its page margin. "The margin
  of the nav bar" is the code's own 16px edge-text rule. Keeping containers
  at the nav box width and padding their content by 16px achieves it with
  one number site-wide.
- Consequences: text measure becomes 916-32 = 884 (was 852 after DR-0001,
  836 before it) - styleguide width copy updated to 916/884. DR-0001's
  "containers span the nav box" is revised to "containers span the nav box,
  content sits 16px inside". The photogrid and full-bleed media stay
  full-bleed (image bleed exception).
