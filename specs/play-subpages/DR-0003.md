# DR-0003: The Play entry is a normal card at the bottom of the index work gallery - not a separate thinner block

- Date: 2026-11-14
- Status: accepted (Pedro, 2026-11-14: "just add Play to the bottom of the gallery, not separated from it"). Supersedes the placement half of DR-0001 (a separate thinner card below the gallery); DR-0001's route decisions (`/play/blog/`, `/play/photography/`) are unaffected.
- Context: Ticket 2 first shipped a separate `.play-teaser` block below `#work` containing a 112px `card--thin` card. Pedro's visual review: it "messes with the background and hiding the text". Measured on the shipped build (Playwright, 1440 + 390): the `.card-title` block is ~118px tall (16px padding + h2 + p) inside the 112px card; being absolutely positioned at `bottom: 0`, it overflows the card's top edge and paints over the gallery's gradient background above the card.
- Options weighed: (a) keep the separate thin block and shrink the title padding until it fits 112px - rejected: fragile text-fitting inside a fixed-height card, more special-case CSS to maintain; (b) render the Play card inside the work gallery through the gallery organism's existing `showPlay` mechanism, as a normal 256px card - chosen.
- Decision: (b). The index passes `showPlay: true` (and keeps `showBlog: false`); the gallery's built-in `_playCard` renders at the bottom of the work gallery (before the bottom-flagged closet card), full size, copy "Play" / "Stuff I do for fun", href `../play`. The `thin` flag, `.card--thin`, `.play-teaser` and the `card-thin.test.js` guard are removed.
- Rationale: one card pattern, no new sizes; the card markup is byte-identical in shape to the work cards, so the title sits inside the image as designed and the gallery's own background/fade flow is untouched. Simplest structure that fixes the reported bug.
- Consequences: the mirror strip (`stripPlayTeaser` in `scripts/mirror-link.js`) now targets only the `<a id="play-teaser">` anchor inside the gallery (no wrapper to remove). Index card order: work cards, Play card, then bottom-flagged cards (closet stays last, ticket 25 amend). The Play card img defaults to an existing shipped thumb (ASSUMPTION - Pedro; H2).
