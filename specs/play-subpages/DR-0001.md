# DR-0001: Both sub-pages live under /play/; the index links to Play with one thinner card below the gallery

- Date: 2026-11-14
- Status: accepted (Pedro's rules 2026-11-14: "they exist in the Play sub-page", "a thinner card below gallery leading to it in index")
- Context: two vestigial stubs exist - `src/blog/index.nunjucks` (depth 1, renders a hero from a `site.data.play-hero` key that does not exist) and `src/play/play_photography/index.nunjucks` (depth 2, renders the word "test"). The gallery organism also has built-in Play/Blog cards (`_playCard`/`_blogCard`, gated by `showPlay`/`showBlog`; the index currently passes both as false).
- Options weighed: (a) reuse the stub routes `/blog/` + `/play/play_photography/`; (b) both sub-pages under `/play/` as `/play/blog/` + `/play/photography/`, with the index entry as one thinner card below the work gallery; (c) put the Play card back inside the index gallery via `showPlay:true`.
- Decision: (b). Routes: `/play/blog/`, `/play/blog/<slug>/`, `/play/photography/`. The index keeps `showPlay:false, showBlog:false` and renders one thinner card below the `#work` gallery linking to `../play`. The Play page's gallery list (`play.json`) becomes exactly two cards: Blog, Photography.
- Rationale: Pedro's rule places blog and photography inside Play (personal/fun content sits next to the other play cards), and the index entry is explicitly "below gallery" as a thinner card, not another full-size work card. One parent keeps the index gallery purely work.
- Consequences: baseUrl depths - `/play/blog/` and `/play/photography/` use `../../`, `/play/blog/<slug>/` uses `../../../`. Ticket 1 removes the two stub dirs (placeholders, unlinked after `play.json` changes) and greps that nothing references `/blog/` or `play_photography` anymore. The built-in `_playCard`/`_blogCard` machinery in the gallery organism stays as-is (still disabled on the index); the mirror/release pipeline is untouched (play pages are public-only).
