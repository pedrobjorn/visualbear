# DR-0004: Play-section heroes are snug (32vh, text centered in the hero); photography controls sit between title and grid

- Date: 2026-11-17
- Status: accepted (pending build)
- Context: after T3's first iteration (`.hero--snug` 32vh) was reverted per "make the title lower as it was", Pedro reports "I see no change" - the hero ended up byte-identical to the portfolio pages. He then specs it: "make the hero smaller, so the text is still in the middle of the hero between the header and the body, but smaller than the portfolio pages. Also put the grid controls between the title and content, not beneath the headline."
- Options weighed: (a) shrink the hero box with the text centered in it (align-items: center stays, title lands at the hero's middle) - chosen; (b) shrink only the spacer and keep a 40vh hero - rejected, Pedro explicitly wants the hero itself smaller than the portfolio 40vh, and a 40vh hero IS the portfolio look he says shows no change; (c) move the hero below the header (top: 48px) - rejected, "between the header and the body" describes the hero's region, not a new offset, and moving it would shift every hero's scroll-mask geometry.
- Decision:
  - `hero.scss`: `.hero--snug { height: 32vh }`, declared BEFORE the mobile mq so mobile `height: auto` still wins. Wired on `/play/` and `/play/blog/` (`class:'hero-about hero--snug'`) and on `/play/photography/` as `class:'hero--snug hero--center'` (replaces the wired-but-unshipped `hero--roomy` - the roomy 48vh idea is dropped, Pedro wants smaller, not bigger). Portfolio pages (`/`, case studies) keep the default 40vh. The hero keeps `align-items: center`, so the title sits at the middle of the 32vh hero - "in the middle of the hero".
  - Spacers keep a 16vh gap under the fixed 32vh hero (was the standard 26vh): `body.page-play .content.gallery { margin-top: 48vh }` + mobile zero-override after it (covers /play/ + /play/blog/).
  - Photography: the spacer moves from the gallery to the `.photoview` container - `body.play-photos .photoview { margin-top: 48vh }` + mobile zero-override - so the `.photoview-switch` (first child) renders BETWEEN the hero title and the photogrid at rest instead of pinned beneath the header. `body.play-photos .content.gallery:has(.gallery-grid) { margin-top: 0 }` (0,4,1) keeps the page-play 48vh rule off the gallery so the spacer is not doubled. The switch keeps `position: sticky; top: 80px` (T6): in flow at rest below the hero; on scroll it sticks 32px under the 48px header and still hides on scroll-down (photoview.js untouched).
- Rationale: 32vh is the value already proposed in plan.md open questions; the hero is invisible ground (the body gradient paints it), so what the user sees is exactly "title height + distance to content" - smaller hero with centered text plus a 16vh gap delivers both "smaller than portfolio" and "less distance from title to content" without moving the title out of the hero's middle.
- Consequences: T3's reverted snug attempt is superseded by this; T6's 32px header gap now applies to the STUCK state on scroll (at rest the controls sit below the hero by design). Tests in play-hero.test.js / photoview.test.js assert the new contracts. Mobile is unchanged in behavior (static hero, zero spacers, switch flows after the hero).
- Follow-up (same day, Pedro: "center the title vertically in the container"): with the hero at 32vh the content can be TALLER than the hero (e.g. /play/'s 3-line list: 372px in a 288px hero), and the flex item's auto margins clamp to 0 on overflow - the title sat at ~64% of the hero instead of the middle. Fixed by centering `.hero--snug .content` absolutely (`top: 50%; transform: translateY(-50%)`; left/right 0 + the global `margin: auto; max-width: 916px` keep the horizontal line centered), reset to `position: static` in the mobile mq. Measured after the fix: h1 center = hero center on both axes at 1440x900 and 1440x700, on /play/, /play/blog/ and /play/photography/.
