# DR-0003: The Photography title and the view controls are centered

- Date: 2026-11-17
- Status: accepted (pending build)
- Context: on `/play/photography/` the title sat left-aligned on the 900px content line (`.photoview-title`) and the grid/full switch was a left-aligned flex row (`.photoview-switch`). The visuAI/Design-system tab strip on /workflow/ is centered (`width: max-content; margin: 32px auto 24px` in tabs.scss). Pedro wants the Photography title and controls centered.
- Options weighed: (a) `text-align: center` on the photos hero via a `hero--center` modifier + `justify-content: center` on `.photoview-switch` - chosen; (b) center everything via the shared hero - rejected, only Photography was asked to be centered; (c) leave the title in place and only center the controls - rejected, the title moves into the hero with DR-0002 so the modifier is the clean place.
- Decision: `.hero--center .content { text-align: center; }` (hero.scss, desktop and mobile alike) and `.photoview-switch { justify-content: center; }` (gallery.scss). Blog's hero stays left-aligned.
- Rationale: the photos page is a full-bleed media grid, so a centered title + centered control strip reads as one composed header row; consistency with the centered vb-tabs strip, which play-consistency T3 will also center (`width: max-content; margin: 0 auto`) - the two changes compose without conflict.
- Consequences: after play-consistency T3 lands, the switch becomes a max-content pill strip that is centered by its margins; `justify-content: center` stays harmless. If the title ever moves out of the hero again, centering must move with it.
