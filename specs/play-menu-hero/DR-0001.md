# DR-0001 (amended): the play NAV menu gets its own variant; the hero and the visualbear nav stay untouched

- Date: 2026-11-17
- Status: accepted - amended same day after Pedro's correction: "no, don't change the hero, change the nav menu. Create a nav menu variant just for play. Do not change the visualbear nav menu. Keep the hero as it was." (First draft had edited the /play/ hero link list in playhero.yml; that was reverted.)
- Context: the site nav is rendered once in `_layouts/base.nunjucks` as `nav.content({links: site.data.sitemap})` (Work/About/Contact). Pedro wants the play section (all 7 pages under `/play/`) to show a play-specific menu: Work and About removed, Blog and Photos added, Contact kept. The visualbear nav must not change anywhere else, and the `/play/` hero keeps its original About/Work link list.
- Options weighed: (a) edit playhero.yml (the first draft) - rejected by Pedro, the hero is not the menu; (b) a per-page `{% set %}` switch - rejected, nunjucks child-set visibility in the parent layout is subtle and implicit; (c) wrap the nav in `{% block nav %}` in base.nunjucks (default `site.data.sitemap`, so every non-play page is byte-identical) and override it in two thin play layouts - chosen.
- Decision: `base.nunjucks` wraps the nav call in `{% block nav %}{{nav.content({links: site.data.sitemap})}}{% endblock %}`. New `src/_data/playsitemap.json`: Blog `/play/blog/`, Photos `/play/photography/`, Contact `#contact`. New layouts `_layouts/play.nunjucks` (extends gallery) and `_layouts/play-work.nunjucks` (extends work, for the blog posts) each override `{% block nav %}` with `site.data.playsitemap`. The 3 gallery play pages extend play.nunjucks; the 4 posts extend play-work.nunjucks. playhero.yml is byte-identical to before. The root-absolute play hrefs work publicly; for mirrored copies under `selected-works/`, `relativizeNavLinks` (scripts/mirror-link.js) now also rewrites `/play/blog/` and `/play/photography/` depth-relative, so the walled-garden release guard holds (it failed on the first build without this, listing the mirrored play pages).
- Rationale: one override point, zero visual change outside `/play/`; the block default keeps the visualbear nav byte-identical on every other page (asserted by test).
- Consequences: any future play page extends a play layout (never gallery/work directly) - enforced by scripts/test/play-menu.test.js. The mirror's play copies carry the relativized play nav and stay inside the air-lock.
